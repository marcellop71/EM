\paragraph{The local archive.}
Results that were proved and later retired---because their premise turned
out to be false, vacuous, or a definitional collapse---are kept in a
directory \code{EM/Archive/} that is \emph{not part of the public
repository}: it is preserved locally, guarded by \code{\#exit}, and
documented there in a README (the ``RED chain'').  In this paper such
results are cited as \archived{}{name} without a link; the mathematical
content is stated in full where it is used, and the archived Lean text is
available on request.

\subsection{Axiom Usage: What's Constructive}

The core definitions (\code{seq}, \code{prod}, \code{aux}) and
their basic properties (\code{seq\_isPrime}, \code{seq\_injective})
are \textbf{fully constructive}: they use only \code{propext} and
\code{Quot.sound} (no \code{Classical.choice}, no
\code{Decidable} instances beyond~$\NN$).  Euclid's theorem
itself (\code{EM/Core/Euclid.lean}) is constructive.

Classical reasoning enters at the reduction level:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item The $\HH \Rightarrow \MC$ proof uses well-founded induction
  (strong induction on $\NN$), which in Lean~4 is constructive but
  relies on \code{Classical.choice} for the
  cofinal-implies-hit argument.
\item Character theory (orthogonality, Fourier inversion) is
  inherently classical via \code{open Classical}.
\item All open hypotheses are stated as \code{def \ldots : Prop},
  never as \code{sorry}'d theorems.  The type-checker guarantees
  that no proof obligation is silently assumed.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{ANT Formalization Status}
\label{sec:ant-formalization}

The analytic chain, as far as it is true, is
\[
  \underbrace{L(1,\chi) \neq 0}_{\text{Mathlib}}
  \;\xrightarrow[\text{\small \checkmark}]{\text{Karamata}}\;
  \text{WeightedPNTinAP}^{\ast}
  \;\xrightarrow[\text{\small \checkmark}]{\text{strip}}\;
  \text{PrimeLogSum}^{\ast}
  \;\xrightarrow[\text{\small \checkmark}]{\text{Abel}}\;
  \text{PrimesEquidist}^{\ast}
\]
where ``\checkmark'' denotes machine-verified arrows and the asterisk
marks the \emph{asymptotic} forms of the classical statements
(error $o(\log x)$, resp.\ $o(\log\log x)$, in place of the $O(1)$
of Iwaniec--Kowalski Theorem~2.2 and~(2.25)).  Every arrow is a
theorem: the chain carries no hypothesis.  Earlier drafts continued it
by an ``Alladi'' arrow to MFRE and PE; that continuation is not open but
refuted---\code{EM/Population/HeadDomination.lean} proves the unshifted
MFRE \emph{equivalent} to a false identity between convergent series
(Dead End~\#160, \S\ref{sec:ant-def})---and its scaffolding is archived
(\code{EM/Archive/Population/AlladiDensityArchive.lean} (local archive)).
Three files provide the ANT infrastructure:
\code{EM/IK/Tauberian.lean} (730~lines, zero \code{sorry})
proves the $L$-series bounds and the stripping step, and introduces the
asymptotic forms;
\code{EM/IK/AbelChain.lean} (864~lines, zero \code{sorry}) proves the
discrete Abel summation chain converting log-weighted prime
sums into reciprocal-weighted sums, in both the $O(1)$ and asymptotic
forms; and
\code{EM/IK/Karamata.lean} (670~lines, zero \code{sorry}) proves
Karamata's Tauberian theorem for Dirichlet series with nonnegative
coefficients and applies it to obtain WeightedPNTinAP$^{\ast}$
unconditionally.  A fourth, \code{EM/Population/HeadDomination.lean}
(${\sim}830$~lines, zero \code{sorry}), settles what the chain feeds
into.

\paragraph{Proved steps.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{One-sided Tauberian lemma}
(\lean{EM/IK/Tauberian.lean}{one\_sided\_tauberian\_upper}):
for nonnegative $b_n \geq 0$,
$\sum_{n \leq N} b_n \leq N^\varepsilon \cdot \sum_n b_n / n^\varepsilon$.
Elementary proof via $n^\varepsilon \leq N^\varepsilon$.
This gives $O(N^\varepsilon)$ partial-sum bounds from Dirichlet series
behavior, without requiring the full Wiener--Ikehara theorem.

\item \textbf{L-series two-sided bounds}
(\lean{EM/IK/Tauberian.lean}{residueClass\_tsum\_both\_bounds}):
Mathlib's \code{PrimesInAP.lean} proves a \emph{lower} bound on
$\sum \Lambda(n)\mathbf{1}_{n \equiv a}/n^s$ near $s = 1$
via \code{LSeries\_residueClass\_lower\_bound}, but does not export
the corresponding upper bound.  We extract the identity
$\mathrm{tsum} = \mathrm{Aux}(a,s) + (\varphi(q))^{-1}/(s{-}1)$
from Mathlib's proof and derive both bounds.

\item \textbf{Prime power stripping}
(\lean{EM/IK/Tauberian.lean}{prime\_power\_stripping\_proved}):
$\text{WeightedPNTinAP} \Rightarrow \text{PrimeLogSumEquidist}$.
The von Mangoldt function $\Lambda$ counts prime powers
$p^k$ with weight $\log p$; the contribution from $k \geq 2$
is uniformly bounded by $\sum_p 2\log p / p^2 < \infty$.
This 250-line proof strips the higher prime powers and
converts the $\Lambda$-weighted sum into a $(\log p)/p$
sum over primes only.

\item \textbf{Abel summation chain}
(\lean{EM/IK/AbelChain.lean}{prime\_log\_to\_reciprocal\_proved},
641~lines):
  $\text{PrimeLogSumEquidist} \Rightarrow
  \text{PrimesEquidistributedInAP}$.
  Discrete Abel summation (summation by parts) converts the
  log-weighted prime sum $\sum (\log p)/p$ into the
  reciprocal-weighted sum $\sum 1/p$.
  The proof includes derivative identities
  $(\log\log t)' = 1/(t\log t)$ and
  $(-1/\log t)' = 1/(t(\log t)^2)$; integral evaluations via FTC;
  the sandwich bound
  $|\sum_{k=2}^{m-1}(\log(k{+}1) - \log k)/\log(k{+}1)
  - (\log\log m - \log\log 2)| \leq 1/(\log 2)^2$;
  and the discrete Fubini step (double-sum reindexing).

\item \textbf{Full composed chain}
(\lean{EM/IK/AbelChain.lean}{wpnt\_implies\_primes\_equidist\_proved}):
  $\text{WeightedPNTinAP} \Rightarrow
  \text{PrimesEquidistributedInAP}$,
  composing prime power stripping with discrete Abel summation.

\item \textbf{The $O(1)$ is not load-bearing.}
  Nothing downstream of the ANT chain consumes a rate: the population
  statements it was meant to feed are \code{Filter.Tendsto} statements
  about ratios (now known false, but the point about rates stands).
  Both chain steps survive the weakening of the input error from
  $O(1)$ to $o(\log x)$
  (\lean{EM/IK/Tauberian.lean}{prime\_power\_stripping\_asymp\_proved},
  \lean{EM/IK/AbelChain.lean}{prime\_log\_to\_reciprocal\_asymp\_proved}).
  The Abel step is the one with content: the telescoped remainder is
  split at a threshold $J(\varepsilon)$, below which it is a constant
  and above which $\varepsilon\log j\cdot(1/\log j - 1/\log(j{+}1))$
  is exactly $\varepsilon$ times the summand of the main sum, so the
  tail is $\leq \varepsilon\,\log\log x$.
  This measurement is what makes the next item the right target: a
  Tauberian theorem delivers the asymptotic form and not the $O(1)$.

\item \textbf{Karamata's Tauberian theorem}
(\lean{EM/IK/Karamata.lean}{Karamata.karamata}).
  For $c_n \geq 0$ with $\sum_n c_n n^{-s}$ convergent for $s > 0$
  and $s\sum_n c_n n^{-s} \to C$ as $s \to 0^{+}$, one has
  $\sum_{n \leq x} c_n \sim C\log x$.
  The proof is the classical one with the Laplace--Stieltjes transform
  replaced by the Dirichlet series and $t = \log n$: the limit is
  computed for monomials $y^k$ (it is $C/(k{+}1)$, by rescaling
  $s \mapsto (k{+}1)s$), extended to polynomials by linearity, and the
  cutoff $\mathbf 1[n \leq e^{1/s}] = \mathbf 1[n^{-s} \geq e^{-1}]$ is
  sandwiched between $y\,P^{-}(y)$ and $y\,P^{+}(y)$ for Weierstrass
  polynomials $P^{\pm}$ approximating continuous ramps around
  $\mathbf 1[y \geq e^{-1}]/y$, whose integrals over $[0,1]$ are within
  $\eta$ of~$1$.  Nonnegativity of $c_n$ is what makes the pointwise
  sandwich sum.

\item \textbf{WeightedPNTinAP$^{\ast}$, unconditionally}
(\lean{EM/IK/Karamata.lean}{weightedPNTinAP\_asymp\_proved}).
  Karamata applied to $c_n = \Lambda(n)\mathbf 1_{n \equiv a}/n$: the
  Dirichlet series is $\sum \Lambda(n)\mathbf 1_{n\equiv a}/n^{1+s}$, and
  the two-sided $L$-series bound above at $\sigma = 1{+}s$ gives
  $s\,F(s) = 1/\varphi(q) + O(s)$.  Composed with the asymptotic Abel
  chain this yields
  \lean{EM/IK/Karamata.lean}{primesEquidistInAP\_asymp\_proved}:
  $\sum_{p \leq x,\, p \equiv a} 1/p \sim (\log\log x)/\varphi(q)$,
  and the analytic chain is unconditional end to end.

\item \textbf{Dirichlet's theorem}
(\lean{EM/IK/Tauberian.lean}{dirichlet\_primes\_in\_ap}):
imported directly from Mathlib's
\code{Nat.infinite\_setOf\_prime\_and\_eq\_mod}.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Head domination.}
\code{EM/Population/HeadDomination.lean} proves: the density of
$\{m:\minFac(m)=p\}$ is $w_p = p^{-1}\prod_{r<p}(1-1/r)$
(\lean{EM/Population/HeadDomination.lean}{card\_minFac\_eq\_ge}); the
weights telescope, $w_p = c(p) - c(p{+}1)$
(\lean{EM/Population/HeadDomination.lean}{w\_eq\_cfun\_sub}), with
$c \to 0$ (\lean{EM/Population/HeadDomination.lean}{cfun\_tendsto\_zero});
the class density is $\sum_{p\equiv a} w_p$
(\lean{EM/Population/HeadDomination.lean}{tendsto\_classCount\_div});
and RoughLPFEquidist$(q)$ holds iff those sums all equal $c(q{+}1)/(q-1)$
(\lean{EM/Population/HeadDomination.lean}{roughLPFEquidist\_iff}).
A finite set of primes of one class carrying more than the equidistributed
share refutes it
(\lean{EM/Population/HeadDomination.lean}{not\_roughLPFEquidist\_of\_head});
we state, and do not certify by computation, that this happens already
at $q=5$ in the class of~$7$.  UniformConductorEquidist at conductor~$1$
is the same statement
(\lean{EM/Adelic/UniformConductor.lean}{uce\_implies\_roughLPFEquidist}).
The population statement that \emph{is} true is the double limit
$z\to\infty$ after $X\to\infty$; it follows from PrimeLogSum$^{\ast}$
by partial summation, but it is not what any reduction consumed.
Formalizing ANT further does not affect the paper's contribution: the
reduction CME~$\Rightarrow$~MC is what this work contributes.
