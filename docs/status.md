## Headline results (all machine-verified)

1. **CME ⟺ MC.** `cme_implies_mc` (`EM/CME/Reduction.lean`) via CCSB; the converse is
   vacuous (`EM/CME/Equivalences.lean`).  CME is a *reformulation* of MC, never a weaker
   hypothesis; the content is the direction CME ⇒ MC.  The whole walk layer
   (CME, CCSB, DH, SHH, HH, VE, …) is one equivalence class (`walk_layer_equivalences`).
2. **The population layer PE / MFRE / DSL is false** (head domination,
   `EM/Population/HeadDomination.lean`, dead end #160); the old DSL ⇒ CME rung is archived.
3. **Seed-average law, unconditional, natural density**
   (`EM/Population/AlmostAllDensity.lean`): for a fixed prime `q` and `ε > 0` there is a
   horizon `n` such that the seeds `m ≤ X` coprime to `q` that have not selected `q` within
   `n` steps number ≤ `εX` for all large `X` (`almost_all_genmc_density`, `_limsup`);
   corollary: coprime seeds that *never* select `q` have upper density 0
   (`never_captures_limsup_eq_zero`).  Finitely many `q` at a time
   (`finite_simultaneous_density`), and — since the per-range density is exactly zero — a
   range growing with the seed: for some ineffective nondecreasing `Q → ∞`, the seeds `m`
   that never select some prime `q ≤ Q m`, `q ∤ m`, have natural density 0
   (`EM/Population/GrowingRange.lean`, `seed_range_never_density`; the Tao–Collatz
   "almost bounded values" shape).  `GenMC` is invariant under the greedy map
   `m ↦ m·minFac(m+1)` (`genMC_genProd_iff`).  Simultaneous-in-`q` natural density is
   OPEN (#167/#168: finite additivity, not a rate problem; #176: the scale-uniform tail
   input, if demanded for all `X`, is an orbit statement — it implies a cofinite MC).
   **MC ⟺ `prod n → 0` in the profinite topology** (`EM/Population/ProfiniteAttractor.lean`,
   `mc_iff_tendsto_zero`): MC says the seed `2` is in the basin of the attractor `0`.
   **The first coincidence** (`measure_divisorFinite_eq_zero`): μ-a.e. point has infinitely
   many vanishing coordinates, every integer finitely many — integer orbits are an infinite
   sequence of measure-zero coincidences, which is #90 stated structurally.  Descent shape
   on file (`descent_empty`; unusable as stated, `T(ℕ) ⊆ 2ℕ`).
   **Transfer principle** (`EM/Population/GrandOrbit.lean`, `transfer_principle`): for any
   relation under which `Misses q` is invariant, a positive-upper-density class of `2` proves
   MC; instance `grandOrbit_transfer` for "orbits eventually coincide".  Preimage structure
   (`preimage_iff`, square condition `p² ∣ N + p`) says that class is heuristically thin.
   **Adelic shadow** (`EM/Population/AdelicShadow.lean`): every finite place is hit by the
   Euclid numbers only finitely often (`hits_finite`, `eventually_unit`), more than `π(q−1)`
   hits force selection (`captured_of_many_hits`), so MC-failure at `q` is "eventually a
   `q`-adic unit on `P_n` and `E_n`" (`misses_iff_eventually_unit_both`); the growth defect is
   the log of the discarded cofactor (`defect_eq_log_cofactor`).  A height argument for
   capture was attempted and closed (#177, `docs/analysis/height_argument_attempt_2026-08-20.md`):
   the walk mod `q` is the torsion component of `ℤ_q^×`, invisible to every absolute value and
   `q`-adic logarithm; formally the capture identity (`captured_iff_mem_visited`).
   **Every prime is selected by some squarefree seed coprime to it**
   (`EM/Population/SquarefreeSeed.lean`, `exists_squarefree_seed_selects`) — a counting
   corollary: failing seeds + multiples of `q` + non-squarefree (`≤ X/2`) cannot fill `[1,X]`;
   in fact **almost all** and **infinitely many** squarefree seeds coprime to `q` select `q`
   (`almost_all_squarefree_select`, `infinite_squarefree_seeds_select`).
4. **Profinite ensemble** (`EM/Population/ProfiniteHeadline.lean`): on `Ω = ∏_q ℤ/q` with

5. **Floor and obstructions.** MC ⇒ weak MC ⇒ RD ⇒ (S) ⇒ (C∞); growth constant `C` as a

## Frontier

- Simultaneous-in-`q` seed-average law in natural density (§G) — open; the two obvious
  routes are dead (#167/#168), the rate-only input is an orbit statement (#176); the
  countably additive repair is the profinite result above.  By analogy
  (`docs/analysis/analogy_map_2026-08-20.md`) the honest next population target is an
  *effective* growing range `Q(X) ≍ log log log X`, i.e. Mertens in APs with explicit `O(1)`;
  any *existence* statement `∃ m, GenMC m` would need a coupling of failures across distinct
  `T`-orbits (Heath-Brown's mechanism for Artin); made precise by `transfer_principle`: a
  GenMC-preserving relation with a positive-density class of `2` *is* a proof of MC.  The
  grand orbit is the canonical candidate and is presumably thin (mod-`p²` branching).
- The orbit of 2: #90/#117 untouched; the sure (per-path) layer's orbit direction is closed
  (#169–#174, `docs/analysis/sure_layer_missed_primes.md`).
