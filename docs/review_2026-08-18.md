# Challenge review, 2026-08-18 — the repo and the paper as readers would see them

Four independent reviews were run (two Mathlib-user hats on the code, two mathematician hats on
the paper); raw reports in `docs/reviews/`.  Every finding below was re-verified in the source
before inclusion.  Ranked by how much it would cost us with the respective reader.

## A. What both readerships will say first

**A1. The headline "reduction" is an equivalence, and the paper says the opposite.**
`ConditionalMultiplierEquidist` (`EM/CME/Reduction.lean:49`), `ComplexCharSumBound`,
`DynamicalHitting`, `SingleHitHypothesis`, `HittingHypothesis`, `VisitEquidistribution`,
`WindowFourierGain` all begin `∀ q … (hne : ∀ k, seq k ≠ q)`.  MC makes `hne` unsatisfiable, so
MC ⇒ each of them vacuously; with the proved converses, **CME ⇔ CCSB ⇔ DH ⇔ SHH ⇔ MC**.  Not
recorded anywhere in Lean; the paper contradicts it: "VE and CME are genuinely different
conditions (neither implies the other)" (introduction.tex:334), "CME is strictly weaker than
CCSB" (why_its_hard.tex:82), "CME is strictly stronger than Decorrelation"
(the_character_sum_reduction.tex:279), "SHH is strictly weaker … converses need not hold"
(the_residue_walk.tex:539–561), "sharpest sufficient condition known and the single open
hypothesis" (abstract).  The paper *does* apply the vacuity observation to `IC_min` and to the
receptacle "Detection" arrow — but not to its own main theorem.  Also: CME ⇒ MC has a two-line
proof (on the most-visited fibre the class −c⁻¹ has zero mass, so some χ has fibre sum ≥ N/(q−1)²).
Fix: state the equivalences (add `mc_implies_cme`, `cme_iff_mc`, … and publish them); reframe the
character-sum layer as *reformulations of MC in the language of the walk*; delete every
"strictly weaker/stronger" between MC-equivalent statements; drop "sharpest sufficient
condition"/"single open hypothesis"; say what a non-vacuous hypothesis would have to look like
(a statement about the residues of a(n) not conditioned on missingness — the thing the retired
population layer tried and failed to supply).

**A2. "Zero axioms" is false as literally stated.**  64 `native_decide` calls in 9 files
(`Group/SEInstances.lean` 29, `Group/QR.lean` 24, …); `#print axioms` shows 9 of the 348
published declarations inherit `native_decide` axioms (`SylvesterTower.*` via `Mullin.seq_seven`,
`mixed_capture_three` proving `Nat.minFac 3 = 3`, `mixed_*_implies_mixed_mc`,
`tsd_all_implies_mixed_mc`).  Headline chain, Karamata, van der Corput are clean.  `EM.lean:3-4`
claims "zero axioms".  Fix: replace with `decide`/`norm_num` (most are gratuitous), gate
`@[publish]` on `#print axioms` in CI, record axioms in `registry/declarations.json`, reword.

**A3. Two ANT "hypotheses" are content-free or false, and one reaches the registry.**
`BombieriVinogradov` and `ElliottHalberstam` (`LargeSieve/Basic.lean:98,892`) are
`∃ E, E ≤ x/(log x)^A ∧ 0 ≤ E` — provable with `E = 0`; so the registered open point
`BVImpliesMMCSB` is just `MultiModularCSB` and "BV → orbit frontier" contains no BV.
`ArithmeticLargeSieve` (`Basic.lean:75`) sums over all `q ≤ Q` (incl. 0, 1) and all characters with
the primitive-character bound — false (a ≡ 1 refutes it); hence `GaussConductorTransfer`,
`ALSFareyImpliesArithLS` are false and `ArithLSImpliesMMCSB` vacuous.  `EMHasLevelOfDistribution θ`
is trivially true (acknowledged as #96).  Fix: state BV/EH honestly (or delete), fix ALS to
primitive characters and `q ≥ 1`, retire `BVImpliesMMCSB`.

## B. Mathematician readers (paper)

**B1. Over-claiming language.**  "MC is provably at least as hard as a no-infinite-prime-tower
statement … the only unconditional lower bound available" — what is proved is MC ⇒ (C∞) (short)
and (C∞) ⇔ no Sylvester prime tower; every conjecture is "at least as hard" as its open
consequences.  "Complete map".  "The formalization has conclusively shown that every tool
requiring independence … fails" (why_its_hard) — what is checked is that (2,2,3,3)^∞ and
(2,3,2,3)^∞ mod 5 differ.  "Only two decomposition strategies exist / no third route" — a
taxonomy stated as a theorem.  Fix: keep the theorems, delete the rhetoric.

**B2. Uncited / folklore.**  Existence of `C = lim log P_N/2^N` is the standard
doubly-exponential-recurrence fact (Aho–Sloane 1973) — uncited.  `C = 0 ⇔ (C∞)` is two lines.
Autonomous branch `w ↦ w²+w`, `Φ₃` = "prime factors of Sylvester numbers are ≡ 1 mod 6" — half
attributed.  The bag-conditioned 1/q law is Shanks' heuristic made precise (label it so).
The "ergodic gloss" (no invariant probability ⇒ no generic orbit) is vacuous: `T(m) > m` on a
countable set has no invariant probability at all — nothing to do with `C`.  Fix: cite; demote.

**B3. Errors of fact / embarrassing sentences.**  Intro (introduction.tex:59–61) attributes
"infinitely many omissions" for maxFac to Cox–van der Poorten 1968 (that is Booker 2012 /
Pollack–Treviño 2014; CvdP proved specific omissions) — correct elsewhere in the paper.
"it is not a prime power" about the seed 2 (the_ensemble_reduction.tex:806).  "could not be
checked in the offline environment … recorded as a belief" (composite_floor.tex:597–599).
"it was checked numerically at p = 3, 7, 43" of a proved identity (composite_floor.tex:632).
`rem:why-dsl` item 3 and why_its_hard §11.9 cite "for almost all starting points CME holds
(the proved StepDecorrelation ⇒ cancellation chain)" while the paper declares SD false (#156).
Heuristic §4.1 predicts infinitely many prime Euclid numbers; Dead End #91 says ≈2.89.
`log₂log₂P(N) = N + log₂C + o(1)` is off by `log₂ ln 2`.  Level-tower statements need `ℓ ≠ 3`
(the Lean carries it).  Take-all rule "provably misses q ≡ 2 (3)" needs squarefreeness (not in
Lean).  Alladi bib entry likely wrong (duality paper is J. Number Theory 9 (1977) 436–451).
Counts inconsistent across the text (150/160, 24/29, "150+ sessions" vs S307).

**B4. Prose ≠ Lean in the variants/FF chapters.**  "Stochastic FF-MC" / "phase transition
sharp at ε = 0" — the Lean (`FFStochasticMC`, `StochasticMC.lean:162`) says only that the
accumulator degree grows; integer `phase_transition_summary` is `‖χ(g)‖ = 1` + a two-value mean
bound + tree reachability; "Random (uniform): MC status Easy (mixing)" via Diaconis–Shahshahani
is neither formalized nor applicable (steps not i.i.d.; `RandomFactorMC.lean` admits it).
Necklace identity labelled the FF analogue of GRH.  Fix: print the Lean statement next to
every theorem environment in §10; where they diverge, the prose loses.

**B5. The obstruction chapter.**  The "deliberate over-approximation makes the theorem
stronger" remark is inverted (a larger transition relation makes "propagating" *weaker* to
refute); read honestly, the No-Invariant Theorem is "capture under minFac is a residue-class
condition + Dirichlet", one paragraph; the calculus/fragment/guards/anatomy layers (~10 pp)
are definitional.  "any omission proof must first establish an anatomy property", "gap now
closed" re Booker, "the first formal statement separating the two sequences at exactly the
level where their truth values separate" — over-claims.

**B6. Trivialities in theorem boxes.**  Walk–divisibility bridge, Fourier bridge ("the single
most important proved result after DH ⇒ MC"), `prod_squarefree`, telescoping norm ≤ 2,
OCE = CME by `rfl`, ρ ∈ [0,½]∪{1}, size–residue decoupling (a CRT + Dirichlet exercise), the
(ℤ/5)ˣ "witnesses" called "the two fundamental barriers … carry the thesis" (they show that a
multiset of multipliers is not a walk).  Reserve "Theorem" for the half-dozen with content.

**B7. Length, apparatus, disclaimers.**  193 pp; variants 36 pp, min/max 15, why-hard 18,
Lean 17, appendix 34 (dead ends 24, glossary 12); 116 `\defname`, 32 `\abbr`, 30 Properties.
The receptacle chapter "calibrated against Wiles and Hill–Hopkins–Ravenel" concludes one arrow
is MC.  The methodology/agent appendix belongs in a report.  Placeholder `[placeholder]` rows
in the verified-results table prove nothing and should not be there.  The disclaimer "some
misalignment may exist between what is stated here and what is proved" (abstract) undermines
"machine-verified" — either align every statement or drop the badge.  Both mathematician
referees independently recommend: **a 15–25 page paper (walk equivalences stated as
equivalences; the floor with Aho–Sloane cited; head domination with a finite certificate; bag
law as precise Shanks; CvdP omission of 5 machine-checked; Karamata/Mertens; almost-all
factor-tree hitting; min/max asymmetry) + a technical report for the rest.**  Dead-ends
appendix: keep ~25–30 rows meeting "checkable one-line refutation of an approach an outsider
would try"; move the rest online (`docs/dead_ends_catalog.md`).

**B8. Head-domination certificate.**  Both referees want `¬ RoughLPFEquidist 5` certified: it
is a finite positive-term computation (class of 7 exceeds its share after 19 primes; class
share ≈1.36 for q = 5, "twice its share" is only the large-q heuristic).  This is a project
decision: the standing rule is "no numeric certificates"; a referee will read the absence as a
gap.  Recommend a single `decide`/`norm_num` certificate for q = 5, clearly labelled.

## C. Mathlib readers (code)

**C1. Reproducibility.**  `lakefile.lean` path-requires `../CA` and
`../../proofinity/declbuild-meta`; `lake-manifest.json` has SSH-only transitive deps
(`git@github.com:marcellop71/{arrow,zlog}-lean.git`); stale LeanCopilot link flags; `autoImplicit`
not disabled.  `EM.lean` needs CA/DeclbuildMeta/Architect via `Meta/{Registry,Strategies,Blueprint}`.
Fix: separate `lean_lib` for Meta so `EM` depends only on Mathlib; https git requires; drop
link args; `autoImplicit false`.

**C2. Placeholders and landscapes.**  27 `Prop := True` defs (all marked, none in the registry)
but used as conjuncts of theorems (`Meta/Dobrushin.lean:532-544`, GaussEM landscapes);
`InfinitelyManyLargeFactorSets` docstring says "key hypothesis" while body is `True`.  119
landscape/summary conjunction theorems, 19 published — documentation as theorems; Mathlib
would reject every one.  Fix: delete placeholders; turn landscapes into `example`s.

**C3. Registry.**  Hashes are name-based over the type `Prop`, so bodies of open points are not
addressed; `IK.WeightedPNTinAP` (a known theorem; asymptotic form proved) and
`PrimesEquidistAsympImpliesRoughLPF` (documented as a false family) still `@[open_point]`;
`#ca_registry` writes into the source tree during `lake build`; open points equivalent to MC
are not flagged.  Fix: retire the two; add an `equivalent_to`/`status` field.

**C4. Conventions.**  117 files with file-top `open Classical`; 53% of declarations at root
namespace (`cnt`, `eAN`, `or`, `norm_sq_sum_le_card_mul`, …); home-grown `Euclid.IsPrime`/`minFac`
forcing 146 `[Fact (Nat.Prime q)] (hq : IsPrime q)` double hypotheses; global instances
duplicating Mathlib (`OrderTop/BoundedOrder (Subgroup G)`, Schwartz `FourierTransform`,
`Fintype.ofFinite` on Mathlib types, 36 anonymous `Fact (Nat.Prime N)`); 54 `#check` in a library
file (`Meta/DeadEnds.lean`); 47 `*_proved` names; `def Foo : Prop := …` + `theorem foo : Foo`
wrappers around reusable results (`VanDerCorputBound`, `NecklaceIdentity`); 14 `maxHeartbeats`
up to 3.2M; stale docstring paths (`AlladiDensity.lean`, `Proof.lean`, `FFBootstrap.lean`, …).

**C5. What is genuinely reusable** (all verified correct): RotorRouter (Mathlib-only imports),
Karamata + Mertens in APs, `ZMod.dft` Parseval/Plancherel, van der Corput, necklace identity,
`card_coprime_affine_block`, `HasDensityZero` API + `hasDensityZero_prime`, `EM/Core/Euclid.lean`.
They need extraction into Mathlib-only files, Mathlib naming, generalization (`ZMod p` → finite
field; bound 1 → M), and no `Prop` wrappers.
