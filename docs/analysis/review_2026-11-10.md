# Repository review, 2026-11-10

Statement-level review of the live tree and the written record: eight independent read-only
passes (headline theorems, open hypotheses, `Population` structural files, the seed-average
chain, `FunctionField`, `Stochastic`/`Ensemble`/`Obstruction`/dead ends, the analytic layer,
papers and docs), then verification and revision. Proof bodies were not re-read: Lean checks
them. What Lean does not check is whether a statement means what its name and prose say, and
that is what this review is about.

Status column: **fixed** (revised in this pass), **Lean** (refutation or correction now
machine-checked, see `EM/Meta/HypothesisAudit.lean`), **open** (recorded, not yet revised).
The status of each row is kept current as the revision proceeds.

## 0. What held up

* Core definitions (`seq`, `prod`, `MullinConjecture`, `genSeq`, `GenMullinConjecture`).
* CME ⟺ CCSB ⟺ DH ⟺ SHH ⟺ HH ⟺ WE ⟺ WFG ⟺ MC, as recorded.
* The seed-average law in density form (`almost_all_genmc_density`, `_limsup`,
  `never_captures_limsup_eq_zero`, `finite_simultaneous_density`, `seed_range_never_density`,
  `almost_all_squarefree_select`) and the profinite headline: faithful, no hypothesis argument,
  standard axioms only, the Mertens-window input checked by hand.
* Karamata, Mertens in progressions (asymptotic form), Dirichlet density; the restatements of
  Bombieri–Vinogradov, Elliott–Halberstam, the analytic large sieve (round-distance form).
* The 𝔽₅ refutation of FF-MC, the composite floors over 𝔽₂ and for p ≡ 1 (3), the growth
  constant, MC ⇒ WM ⇒ RD ⇒ (C∞).
* The max-variant omission of 5 (unconditional), the no-invariant theorems as stated in Lean,
  the ε-family reformulation (#159), almost-all tree hitting.
* Registry counts, placeholder lint, axiom gate.

## 4. Pattern

The recurring cause in §1 is the same as in the August reviews: a hypothesis written for the
generic case with the degenerate parameter values left in (`k = 0`, `L₀ = 1`, `N = 0`, `Y = 0`,
`ε = 0`, a prime already in the sequence, a seed divisible by the target, repeated list
elements), or a `True` placeholder on one side of an implication that is then named as a bridge.
None of these touches the headline results; all of them touch the count of "open hypotheses"
and the theorems the papers list as conditional reductions.
